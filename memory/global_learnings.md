---

## 📜 Entry Schema
```markdown
### [LRN-YYYYMMDD-SEQ] Concise Lesson Title
- **Origin Project**: `project_name` (Path: `...`)
- **Date Recorded**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **User Feedback / Trigger**: Feedback or repeated instruction that triggered this learning.
- **Root Cause**: Why did the AI exhibit suboptimal behavior prior to this rule?
- **Self-Update Action**: What file, rule, or skill was updated?
- **Permanent Rule**: The non-negotiable rule applied to all future sessions.
```
