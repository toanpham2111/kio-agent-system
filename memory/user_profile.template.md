## 🎨 2. Coding & Design Preferences
- **Visual Evidence & Proof of Delivery**:
  - When completing UI/UX refactors or styling tasks: **AUTOMATICALLY CAPTURE BEFORE AND AFTER SCREENSHOTS**.
  - Embed the side-by-side comparison table directly into the delivery report without needing reminders.
- **Frontend & UI/UX**:
  - Enforce **Anti-Slop**: Avoid generic neon purple gradients, avoid repetitive 3-card bento grids, and avoid empty marketing buzzwords.
  - **Curated Photography Standards**: For hospitality, real estate, travel, culinary, and lifestyle sites — **ALWAYS use high-quality authentic photography** (Unsplash/Pexels), never substitute abstract vector SVG clip-art for real photo imagery.
  - **Form Control UX**: Select arrows must never touch borders (minimum 16–18px safe breathing room).
  - **Responsive Design**: Zero horizontal overflow on any viewport, thoroughly verified down to 360px mobile.
  - **Smooth Scrolling**: When smooth scroll is requested, the exclusive standard is **Lenis Scroll** (`https://lenis.dev/`).
- **WordPress & Full Site Editing (FSE)**:
  - 100% of custom blocks must be dynamic SSR (`render.php`) and fully editable in Gutenberg Admin.
  - Header and Footer must be drag-and-drop Template Parts in the Site Editor.
