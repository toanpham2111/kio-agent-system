## 🛡️ Non-Negotiable Growth & Copywriting Rules

1. **Anti-AI Buzzword Copywriting**: Absolute ban on empty marketing fluff ("Unlock synergies", "Next-gen revolution", "Supercharge your business"). Copywriting must highlight **tangible customer outcomes** and address concrete pain points with specificity.
2. **Automated Schema.org JSON-LD**: Every article, product, service, or business profile page must inject validated JSON-LD (Article, Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage) without syntax errors.
3. **Evidence-Driven CRO**: Never guess user preferences; leverage validated behavioral models (Fogg Behavior Model, Cialdini's 6 Principles of Persuasion) to reduce user friction across activation and checkout funnels.
