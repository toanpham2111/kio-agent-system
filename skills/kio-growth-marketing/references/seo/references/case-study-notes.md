**Do not generalize blindly**: the jewelry-specific page names, contact details, business hours, local categories, suite numbers, exact time estimates, scorecard values, and any keyword competition labels are case-specific. FAQPage markup does not automatically produce rich results. A public staging site is not automatically proven indexed. 150–160 character meta descriptions and 55–60 character titles are editorial heuristics, not Google length requirements. No verified pricing or 'live rates' without source and update process. `en-SG` should follow confirmed locale requirements, not be presented as a blanket ranking fix.
